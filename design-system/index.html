Le Plein · Design system v0.1

Panneau de prix

For drivers comparing gas prices one-handed, a roadside price sign treatment (huge condensed numerals, ink-on-enamel chrome, three price colours) makes the cheapest litre the first thing you see. Everything else stays quiet.

Structure: Material token model + MUI component catalogue. Source of truth: styles.css. Toggle Dev Mode to inspect a component; Copy as prompt gives an implementation brief. Switch Jour / Nuit at top right.

Palette

Chrome is ink on warm enamel; the three price colours are the only saturated hues. Every fill has a paired --on-*; colours used as text use their *-ink. All pairs pass WCAG AA (checked by formula, both themes). Values below are read live from the current theme.

Typography

Barlow, a grotesk drawn after North-American highway signs, in two widths: Barlow Condensed for prices and headlines (the sign), Barlow for reading. Prices always use tabular numerals so columns line up.

Spacing

8px spacing() scale with 4px half-steps, plus semantic roles: related < section < major. Every tap target is at least 48px.

Shape

Chunky and rounded, like enamel sign plates. Cards use r-lg, sheets and dialogs r-xl.

Shadows

Small documented subset of MUI's 0–24: four levels plus a pin shadow. By day a soft plate shadow; by night surfaces separate by tone and a hairline.

Motion & z-index

All motion is under 400ms, never blocks input, and collapses to ~0 when the phone's "reduce motion" setting is on. Breakpoints: 600 / 900 / 1200. Layers: app bar 1100 · drawer 1200 · sheet 1250 · dialog 1300 · snackbar 1400 · tooltip 1500.

Icons

Inline SVG sprite, Material-outlined weight (2px, round joins), drawn in the style of Lucide / Material Symbols. Token-driven: stroke:currentColor. Never emoji.

Price extension

The hero. Condensed, tabular, the tenth of a cent raised and small like on a Québec pump sign. Colour follows the level vs. stations around you, but a price is never colour alone. Digits roll into place on change (360ms).
